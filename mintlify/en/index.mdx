# Manage and serve your landing-page content without redeploying your frontend

LPData is a platform to **manage and serve landing-page content through an API**. Your frontend handles the layout; LPData stores the text, links, numbers, and files shown on the page. To change the content, you update LPData, without touching your code or redeploying your site.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Structure your content">
    Create a Landing Page and send its Content Document: a JSON document shaped the way your frontend expects, where every value is an Editable Field with `value` and `type`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Host your files">
    Upload images and other files as Assets and use the returned URLs in the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Serve the content">
    Your frontend fetches the document from `GET https://api.lpdata.io/landing_pages/<public_id>`, without authentication, and renders the page. See [Read published content](/en/public-content).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update anytime">
    Change the document through the authenticated API. The next read returns the new content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What LPData does and what stays with you

| LPData | Your application |
| --- | --- |
| Stores each Landing Page's Content Document | Defines the document structure and how to present it |
| Hosts the Assets used in the content | Renders layout, styles, and interactions |
| Serves content publicly by Public ID | Fetches the content and builds the page |
| Authenticates who manages the content | Provides an editing experience, if you need one |

LPData is API-only: it has no visual editor and does not host pages.
